
# Makmur Lab v2.1 — Hero Background Guide

The homepage now includes a background image system.

## Current state
- Uses `assets/hero-background.svg` as a polished placeholder visual.
- Easy to replace later with a JPG/PNG or your own photo.

## How to replace it
1. Put your image in `/assets`, for example:
   `hero-makmur.jpg`
2. Open `styles.css`
3. Find the `.hero` rule
4. Replace:
   `url("assets/hero-background.svg")`
   with:
   `url("assets/hero-makmur.jpg")`

## Best image directions
Option A — Professional portrait
- Your photo, formal/smart casual, clean expression, dark or neutral tone

Option B — Workspace / desk
- Laptop, notebook, dashboard screen, working environment

Option C — Industrial / operations feel
- Plant, logistics, warehouse, machine, but not sensitive internal details

## Important
Choose images that:
- are not too busy
- still allow white headline text to be readable
- feel professional, calm, and intentional
