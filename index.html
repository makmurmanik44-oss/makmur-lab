HOW I THINK

Working principles.

These are not slogans. They are operating principles I want my work to be measured against.

01

Make assumptions visible.

A decision becomes stronger when assumptions, constraints, and uncertainty are explicit.

02

Control before correction.

Good governance prevents ambiguity before execution instead of trying to repair it after the fact.

03

Turn recurring problems into systems.

A repeated problem should become a workflow, standard, dashboard, checklist, or rule.

NOW

Current direction.

Growing from analyst to procurement leader.

My current focus is less about doing more transactions and more about improving the quality of systems, decisions, governance, and team execution around procurement.

Building in public — carefully.

I publish frameworks, sanitized case studies, and lessons while keeping supplier pricing, contracts, internal operational details, and confidential company information private.
